USCIS N-400 Field Office Dashboard

Quarterly Form N-400 (Application for Naturalization) volumes by USCIS field office: applications received, approved, denied, and pending at quarter end.

Applications by quarter

Received, approved, and denied counts for the quarter (Naturalization + Military Naturalization combined). These are flow counts — each point covers only that quarter's activity, not a running total.

Pending cases at quarter end

Total pending N-400s as of the last day of each quarter (a running snapshot, not summed across quarters). Note: USCIS pending totals aren't directly derivable quarter-to-quarter because of administrative closures and inter-office transfers not reflected elsewhere in this report.

Approval rate vs. denial rate

Share of decided cases (approved + denied) that quarter, computed from the non-military Naturalization category only. Military naturalization is excluded here because ~15% of its office-level cells are suppressed by USCIS for small counts, which would make a rate computed from it unreliable at the office level.

Efficiency: completions ÷ received

Completions (approved + denied, Total) divided by applications received (Total) that quarter. Values below 1 mean the office completed fewer cases than it received — the backlog grew that quarter; above 1 means it worked down more than came in.

Backlog clearance time (months)

A rough capacity gauge, not a forecast: pending (Total) ÷ completions (approved + denied, Total) × 3 months — roughly how long it would take to clear the current backlog at that quarter's completion pace, if receipts and staffing stayed flat. Actual clearance time depends on future receipt volume and staffing changes.

Source: U.S. Department of Homeland Security, USCIS quarterly Form N-400 performance reports by field office, FY2015 Q1 through FY2026 Q3 (47 quarters). USCIS has changed this report's format several times over that span — spreadsheets (FY2015–2018, FY2022–2026) and PDFs (FY2019–2021) — see the data-quality note above the charts for PDF-derived quarters. "D" indicates a value suppressed by USCIS disclosure standards for small counts; suppressed cells are shown as gaps, not zero. The set of field offices has also changed over time as offices opened, closed, or were renamed; an office is selectable here if it appears in any quarter, and its chart will show gaps for quarters before it opened or after it closed.

Note on FY2026 Q3: the source workbook states its reporting period as "April 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026," which is inconsistent with every other quarter's period and with USCIS's own fiscal-quarter definition. This dashboard treats it as a typo for April 1, 2026 – June 30, 2026 (the correct FY2026 Q3 window) and labels the data point accordingly; the raw text is preserved in the underlying dataset.
